## 摘要

公共空间人员流动治理既需要可推演的情景工具，也需要能解释"人如何选择空间"的实证证据。精细人群仿真以个体物理移动为对象，空间建模与行为标定是两个独立成本中心；存量—流量聚合建模以单元存量与跨单元流量替代个体追踪，降低了建模复杂度，却缺少把真实人的实时决策与主观感知接入回路的位置；与此同时，大语言模型驱动的合成智能体仿真虽有群体规模，却多以纯虚拟主体运行，缺少与真人同场的对照设计。本文提出"存量—流量空间建模驱动的参与式交互仿真"框架：把人的空间选择直接表达为流量项，与背景重力流、合成智能体流量同权叠加，经守恒投影推进系统状态，并把聚合状态回传决策者，形成"呈现状态—采集决策—聚合流量—约束投影—状态回传"的闭环。本文实现了可运行的实验平台：统一决策协议使真人与合成智能体经同一接口接入同一仿真世界，内核在每轮强制执行守恒投影与容量反馈。初步验证显示：内核 24/24 单元测试通过；三种条件各 200 轮仿真的最大总量漂移处于 1e-13 量级，守恒检验全部通过；注入 20 名"人类被试"的空间选择后，喷泉观景区存量上升 82.8%、东北通道上升 161.4%、东侧出口下降 51.6%，说明外生人类决策确实改变了系统演化；对第三方贝叶斯纳什均衡疏散模型的 150 次复现保持了出口时间排序与量级，相对偏差 −2.9%～−6.6%。真人实验与人机一致性检验尚未开展，是本框架后续工作的核心任务。

**关键词**：存量—流量模型；参与式仿真；人在回路；合成智能体；人机协同；公共空间；人员流动治理

## Abstract

Governing pedestrian flow in public space requires both scenario tools for projection and empirical evidence on how people choose where to go. Fine-grained crowd simulation models individual physical movement, which makes spatial modelling and behavioural calibration two separate cost centres. Aggregate stock–flow modelling replaces individual tracking with unit-level stocks and inter-unit flows, sharply reducing modelling complexity, yet it has rarely offered a place for real people's real-time decisions and subjective perceptions inside the simulation loop. Meanwhile, large-language-model (LLM) driven synthetic-agent simulation can reach population scale, but synthetic agents usually run as purely virtual subjects without a same-world human comparison. This paper proposes participatory interactive simulation driven by stock–flow spatial modelling. A person's spatial choice is expressed directly as a flow term, added with equal weight to background gravity flows and synthetic-agent flows, projected onto the conservation-feasible set, and then used to advance the system state; the aggregate state is fed back to decision makers, closing a five-step loop of state presentation, decision collection, flow aggregation, constraint projection, and state feedback. We implement a runnable platform in which a unified decision protocol lets human participants and synthetic agents enter the same world through the same interface, while the kernel enforces conservation projection and capacity feedback every round. Preliminary verification shows that all 24 kernel unit tests pass; across three conditions of 200 rounds each, the maximum total-stock drift is on the order of 1e-13 and all conservation checks pass; injecting the spatial choices of 20 "human participants" raises the fountain area stock by 82.8% and the north-east corridor by 161.4% while lowering the east exit by 51.6%, indicating that exogenous human decisions do change system evolution; and a 150-run external reproduction of a third-party Bayesian-Nash-equilibrium evacuation model preserves the original ranking and magnitude of exit times with relative deviations of −2.9% to −6.6%. Human-participant experiments and human–machine consistency tests have not yet been conducted and are the central next step.

**Keywords**: stock–flow model; participatory simulation; human-in-the-loop; synthetic agents; human–machine collaboration; public space; pedestrian-flow governance
